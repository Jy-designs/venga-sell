    Sell FAQ

    Here is additional information to answer some of the common questions about selling crypto and how to benefit from it with Venga.

    Selling turns your crypto into euros, for example to take profits, cover an expense or rebalance what you hold. Crypto prices are volatile and can go up or down after you sell, so think about your own situation before you decide.

    You can sell the cryptos available in the Venga app, such as BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and more. Open Sell in the app to see the full list.

    Venga is authorised by Spain’s CNMV as a crypto-asset service provider under MiCA, and custody is secured by Ledger Enterprise. You see the price and the fees before you confirm each sale.

    You can sell from the Venga app on your phone whenever you’re connected. Your account needs to be verified, and the service is available in the countries listed in our Terms of Service.
